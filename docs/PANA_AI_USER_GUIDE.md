## 1. For Visitors & Prospective Students

If you are browsing the website or looking to upskill, PANA AI helps you find the right courses, understand certifications, and register easily.

### Sample Prompts to Try:
- **Course Recommendations:**
  > *"Which engineering course is best for a beginner in oil & gas instrumentation?"*  
  > *"I work in electrical maintenance. What certifications do you offer for high-voltage systems?"*  
  > *"What courses cover Energy Transition, Carbon Capture, and Renewable Energy?"*
- **Certificates & Accreditations:**
  > *"Are PANA Institute certificates verifiable by employers?"*  
  > *"How do I verify a certificate with its ID number?"*  
  > *"What international standards (ISO, IEC, SAE) are your courses aligned with?"*
- **Training Delivery & Corporate Packages:**
  > *"Do you offer online virtual classes or only physical classroom labs?"*  
  > *"Can my company request tailored on-premise training for 20 engineers?"*  
  > *"How can I register and pay for an on-demand course?"*
- **Contact & Office Information:**
  > *"What is the phone number and address of your Abuja office?"*  
  > *"Where is your Port Harcourt training hub located?"*

## 2. For Enrolled Students

As a student, PANA AI acts as your **24/7 personal co-instructor and study guide**. You can ask technical questions about course modules, get study tips, and navigate your student dashboard.

### Sample Prompts to Try:
- **Technical Concept Explanations:**
  > *"Can you explain how a 4-20mA current loop works in industrial instrumentation?"*  
  > *"What is the difference between HAZOP and FMECA in plant safety analysis?"*  
  > *"Explain cavitation in centrifugal pumps and how to prevent it."*  
  > *"What are the key safety steps before performing hot work in a hazardous area?"*
- **Study & Assignment Assistance:**
  > *"Give me 5 practical study tips for preparing for the Process Control exam."*  
  > *"Help me understand the formula for calculating pump Net Positive Suction Head (NPSH)."*  
  > *"Can you give me a real-world scenario of predictive maintenance using vibration analysis?"*
- **Student Dashboard Navigation:**
  > *"Where can I see my enrolled courses and lesson progress?"*  
  > *"How do I submit my module assignment?"*  
  > *"Where can I download my verified completion certificate?"*

## 4. For Administrators (Interactive Action Controls)

When you are logged in as an **Administrator**, PANA AI provides **superpowers to update the live website directly from the chat** without opening complex database tools.

### A. Update Website Text, Phone Numbers & Addresses
Simply tell PANA AI what text to update. An interactive **Website Update Action Card** will appear with a confirmation button.
- **Sample Prompts:**
  > *"Update the Abuja phone number to +234 702 600 0000"*  
  > *"Change the central email to admissions@pana-institute.com"*  
  > *"Update the About Us mission statement to: Delivering world-class technical engineering training."*

### B. Update Course Prices Using Course Code
PANA AI recognizes course codes (like `SC05`, `EX13`, `PD01`) and will update course pricing directly in the database.
- **Sample Prompts:**
  > *"Change SC05 price to 50000"*  
  > *"Update the course fee for EX13 to 75000"*  
  > *"Set course PD01 price to 0 (Free)"*

### C. Change Hero Banners & Website Images
You can provide an image link or click the **paperclip/upload button** in the chat input to upload a photo directly from your phone or computer.
- **Sample Prompts:**
  > *"Replace the homepage hero banner"*  
  > *"Update the website header logo"*  
  > *"Change the course thumbnail for SC05"*  
  > *"I uploaded this image, assign it to the Contact page header"*

### D. Upload a Course Brochure & Publish Instantly
Have a course flyer, PDF document, or syllabus? Upload it directly into the chat or ask PANA AI to extract it:
- **Sample Prompts:**
  > *"Here is the brochure for EX13 Reliability Centred Maintenance. Extract the modules and publish the course."*  
  > *"Publish course EX14: Subsea Pipeline Engineering, 32 hours, advanced level with 4 day modules."*
- **What happens:**  
  PANA AI extracts the course title, code, level, duration hours, standards, OEM partners, curriculum breakdown, and links your brochure as the thumbnail. Click **"Confirm & Publish Course to Website"** and it will be live immediately in the public [Course Catalog](/courses).

### E. Publish News & Press Releases
- **Sample Prompts:**
  > *"Publish a news article: PANA Institute signs corporate training partnership with Baker Hughes for asset integrity training across West Africa."*  
  > *"Draft and post a press release announcing our new 2026 Energy Transition programs."*
